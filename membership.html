Membership, Chapters & Linkages

Join Space Tourism Society Africa or establish a university chapter.

Membership is open across all African countries to secondary and university students, academic researchers, aviation and travel professionals, and private-sector institutions—irrespective of discipline, gender, religion, or nationality.

Eligibility & Pathways

Three ways to participate.

01

Student & Young Professional Members

Open from secondary school through OND, HND, B.Sc, M.Sc, and Ph.D levels across science, engineering, law, media, business, and hospitality departments.

02

University Campus Chapters

Faculty advisers and student leaders may charter an official campus chapter—following established models at Lead City University, Afe Babalola University, and Ajayi Crowther University.

03

Corporate, Travel & Agency MoUs

Coordinated through the Directorate of Linkages & MoU for travel associations, insurers, airlines, embassies, and space research centers.
