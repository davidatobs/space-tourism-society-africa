Programmes & Delegations

Twelve charter activities connecting African campuses to global space centers.

From weekly university radio broadcasts in Ibadan to international study missions in Huntsville, Dubai, Accra, and Tokyo, every STSA activity bridges academic learning with practical industry exposure.

2026 – 2027 Cycle

Current & upcoming international initiatives.

Late 2026 · Abuja & Nationwide

International Space Week Partnership

Collaborative university programming co-hosted with Nile University of Nigeria, alongside ongoing carrier sponsorship discussions with Royal Air Maroc to expand student and faculty mobility.

Q4 2026 · Japan

JAXA Educational Student Visit

Coordinated study visit taking selected Nigerian university students to facilities of the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA), building on STSA’s 20-year link with Japan since IAC Fukuoka 2005.

Apply for student cohort →
2027 Launch · Europe & Africa

European Space Internship Pathway

Cross-continental internship initiative placing students from multiple African countries inside European space organizations while structuring return pathways that counter continental brain drain.

No. Charter Activity Target Participants Scope & Partner Institutions
01 Seminars, Symposia & Continental Conferences Universities, policymakers, researchers Academic colloquia, World Space Week symposiums, and Space Renaissance lectures across African campuses.
02 The Space Party University students & young professionals Flagship social and cultural space gathering inspired by international Yuri’s Night celebrations.
03 Space & Sky Viewing Nights Schools, campuses & general public Optical telescope stargazing, sidewalk astronomy, and IAU community outreach across schools and IDP camps.
04 Space Equipment Handling Workshops S.T.E.A.M. undergraduates & educators Practical training on astronomical telescopes, DTH satellite receivers, and atmospheric sensors.
05 Local & International STS Activities All registered chapter members Coordinated participation in Space Tourism Society global campaigns, Space Tourism Day (April 28), and partner forums.
06 Local, Regional & International Space Tours Students, faculty, NANTA & corporate executives Facility tours from CSTP Epe (Lagos) and CAR–NASRDA to Benin, Togo, Ghana, Huntsville Space Camp, and JAXA.
07 STS International Headquarters Events Regional delegates & industry sponsors Representation at Space Tourism Society Headquarters conferences in Los Angeles, California, and Colorado.
08 Regional & National Space Competitions Secondary schools & university teams Design, essay, and innovation challenges discovering engineering and space-business talent across Africa.
09 Mr. & Miss Space Tourism Society University chapter ambassadors Inter-chapter student leadership and science-communication ambassadorship across African campuses.
10 CAR–NASRDA Supervising Partnership Programme Research students & faculty fellows Joint capacity-building with the Centre for Atmospheric Research of Nigeria’s National Space Research and Development Agency.
11 Annual Dinner & Space Leadership Awards Members, diplomatic missions & patrons End-of-year convocation recognizing African space researchers, university administrators, and industry partners.
12 International Exhibition Projects Student engineering & media teams Preparing African student payloads, papers, and exhibits for global showcase at the International Astronautical Congress (IAC).
